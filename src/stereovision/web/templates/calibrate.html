Tips for a good calibration
  • Aim for 20–30 pairs. Green corner counts mean the pair can be saved.
  • Cover the whole image: board in the centre, all four corners and the edges.
  • Vary the distance (about 0.5–1.5 m) and tilt the board up to ~45° in different directions.
  • Hold still while capturing; motion blur ruins the corners.
